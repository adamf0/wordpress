                “Earthquake Resistant Building Construction: Structural, Material, and Technological Innovations for Safe and Sustainable Buildings.”
            </p>
            <p>
                PT Iida Group Holdings, in an interview with Konstruksi Media, reaffirmed its commitment to advancing earthquake-resistant construction solutions in Indonesia. Recognizing Indonesia’s position along the Pacific Ring of Fire, the company introduces IG Strong CB, where walls function as the primary structural element rather than mere partitions. Through our flagship product, IG Strong CB, we deliver precision-engineered concrete blocks with integrated joint systems that distribute seismic forces more evenly, enhancing structural resilience while improving construction efficiency compared to conventional methods.
            </p>
        </div>
    </div>
    <p>
        Beyond material innovation, we are building a sustainable construction ecosystem through our production facility in Gunung Putri, Bogor, training programs, and strategic collaborations with housing developers across Indonesia. IG Strong CB offers additional benefits such as sound insulation, energy efficiency, and fire resistance.
    </p>
    <p>
        With an eco-friendly approach and technology transfer from Japan, PT Iida Group Holdings aims to reshape the property industry mindset—positioning homes not merely as living spaces, but as a foundation for safety, comfort, and long-term sustainability.

<div class="space-y-6 text-gray-700 leading-relaxed text-base md:text-lg">
    <p>
        <strong class="text-gray-900">Jakarta &amp; Bogor, September 24–25, 2025</strong> – PT Iida Group Holdings successfully held a two-day event themed <strong class="text-primary">“Membangun Masa Depan Teknologi IG Strong CB: Solusi Konstruksi Aman Ramah Lingkungan dan Efisien.”</strong>
    </p>
    <p>
        The event was organized to introduce <strong class="text-gray-900">IG Strong CB</strong>, an earthquake-resistant housing technology, to contractors, construction consultants, and local government representatives from Jakarta and surrounding regions.
    </p>
    
    <div class="border-l-4 border-primary pl-4 py-1 my-6 space-y-4">
        <h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-gray-900">September 24, 2025 – Talk Show and Expert Sharing</h3>
        <p>
            The first day, held at <strong class="text-gray-900">Morrissey Hotel, Jakarta</strong>, featured a talk show session with distinguished guest speakers:
        </p>
        <ul class="list-disc list-inside space-y-1 pl-2">
            <li><strong>Dr. Ir. Maryoko Hadi, Dipl.E.Eng, MT</strong></li>
            <li><strong>Dr. Ir. Hari Nugraha Nurjaman, MT</strong></li>
            <li><strong>Dr. Ir. Drs. Edison H. Manurung, ST, MT, MM, MH</strong></li>
        </ul>
        <p>
            In addition, our own team members <strong class="text-gray-900">Mr. Erich Billy Dumais, Mr. Julian Fikar</strong>, and <strong class="text-gray-900">Mr. Zulkifli Puteh</strong> contributed to the discussions. They shared valuable insights on the opportunities and challenges of implementing earthquake-resistant housing in Indonesia, as well as how IG Strong CB can support safer, more efficient, and sustainable housing development.
        </p>
    </div>

    <div class="border-l-4 border-primary pl-4 py-1 my-6 space-y-4">
        <h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-gray-900">September 25, 2025 – Factory Visit, Training, Hands-On Demonstration, and Certification</h3>
        <p>
            On the second day, participants visited PT Iida Group Holdings’ <strong class="text-gray-900">Factory and Training Center in Gunung Putri, Bogor</strong>. The program combined theoretical learning with practical demonstrations, giving attendees the opportunity to experience firsthand the installation process and explore the efficiency, durability, and eco-friendly benefits of IG Strong CB technology.
        </p>
        <p>
            In addition, participants were assessed by certified evaluators to obtain <strong class="text-primary">BNSP certification as Intermediate Building Structure Supervisors</strong> (Pengawas Pekerjaan Struktur Bangunan Gedung Madya).
        </p>

<div class="space-y-6 text-gray-700 leading-relaxed text-base md:text-lg">
    <p>
        <strong class="text-gray-900">Iida Group Holding</strong> has established cooperation with the <strong class="text-gray-900">University of Tokyo</strong>, <strong class="text-gray-900">Bandung Institute of Technology (ITB)</strong>, and <strong class="text-gray-900">Bogor Agricultural University (IPB)</strong> to conduct joint research into the development of timber houses. The research aims to promote earthquake-resistant, durable, and energy-efficient timber houses.
    </p>
    <p>
        The inauguration of the collaboration was attended by various important figures, including the President of Iida Group HD, <strong class="text-gray-900">Masashi Kanei</strong>, and the rectors and professors of the institutions involved. Over the next three years, the project will focus on developing model houses, termite-resistant wooden components, analytical surveys of local building materials and structures, and carbon-neutral housing schemes in Indonesia.
    </p>
